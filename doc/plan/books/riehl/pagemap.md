## 5. Numbering

**One shared counter per SECTION** — `chapter.section.item` — running across
**Definition / Theorem / Proposition / Lemma / Corollary / Example / Remark /
Notation *and also across numbered displayed equations and diagrams*.** It is not
per-chapter and not per-kind. The counter resets at each section.

### Proving example (read from the page, PDF 102–103 = printed 82–83)

```
§3.1  Limits and colimits as universal cones
  DEFINITION 3.1.1.   constant functor / constant diagram functor Δ
  DEFINITION 3.1.2.   cone over a diagram, summit/apex, legs
       (3.1.3)        ← NUMBERED DISPLAY: commuting triangle Fj → Fk under c
       (3.1.4)        ← NUMBERED DISPLAY: the ω-shaped cone diagram
  DEFINITION 3.1.5    (limits and colimits I)
  DEFINITION 3.1.6    (limits and colimits II)
  PROPOSITION 3.1.7   (essential uniqueness of limits and colimits)
```

`3.1.3` and `3.1.4` **are displays, not environments.** An inventory that expects them
to be a Definition or Theorem will fail to find them; one that reads them as
environments will fabricate items. Corroborating: §4.1 runs Def 4.1.1, (4.1.2),
Lemma 4.1.3, (4.1.4), Notation 4.1.5; §2.2 runs Ex 2.2.1, Ex 2.2.2, Prop 2.2.3,
Thm 2.2.4 (Yoneda), (2.2.5), (2.2.6), Rmk 2.2.7, Cor 2.2.8; §6.2 runs Thm 6.2.1,
(6.2.2)–(6.2.5), Def 6.2.6, Cor 6.2.7 … (6.2.10)–(6.2.11) … Cor 6.2.14, Ex 6.2.15.

### Environment kinds

Only these appear: **Definition, Theorem, Proposition, Lemma, Corollary, Example,
Remark, Notation, Exercise**. There is no Digression / Observation / Convention /
Construction / Warning / Scholium environment. Kind names are set in SMALL CAPS
(`DEFINITION 1.1.1.`). Proofs are `PROOF. … ▫` and are unnumbered; some are one line
deferring to an exercise (“PROOF. Exercise 1.1.ii.”). Many items carry a parenthetical
title after the number — `DEFINITION 3.1.5 (limits and colimits I)`,
`THEOREM E.2.2 (Mac Lane, Kelly)` — which must not be confused with a display tag.
A few numbers are cited in the plural (“Lemmas 3.4.5 and 3.4.6”).

### The Epilogue

Same `c.s.n` scheme with `E` in the chapter slot. **§E.1 introduces no new numbered
items:** it restates ten main-text theorems with their ORIGINAL numbers in
parentheses after an unnumbered kind word —
`THEOREM (2.2.4, 2.2.8).`, `THEOREM (4.7.17).`, `THEOREM (5.5.1, 5.6.1, 5.6.5, 5.6.12).`
These must not be inventoried as `E.1.x`. Genuinely new items live only in E.2–E.5:

## 6. Warnings

**Offset traps**

1. `printed = pdf − 20` holds **only from PDF 21**. Front matter is roman with offset 0
   (`PDF n = roman n`). Never extend +20 backwards.
2. All seven blank pages are **counted**. Six are trailing chapter versos and are
   included in the ranges above, so each of Ch. 1, 2, 3, 5, 6 and the Epilogue ends on
   a blank page. Expected, not a rendering failure.
3. **Chapter 4 has no blank verso** — printed 180 (PDF 200) is a full text page and
   Chapter 5 opens on the very next leaf. It is the only such boundary.

**Extraction traps**

4. **Alt-text before the running head.** Accessibility alt-text paragraphs for figures
   are emitted ahead of the running head in reading order on roughly 16 pages
   (PDF 25, 38, 40, 41, 50, 53, 97, 106, 110, 117, 118, 122–124, 132, 138). Taking
   “the first line of `pdftotext`” as the folio returns alt-text on those pages. Use
   images, or scan the whole page — the folio is always present.
5. **Folio position varies.** On every chapter/section opener (PDF 21, 73, 101, 151,
   201, 239, 271, 281, 285, 287, 289) there is no running head and the folio is
   centered at the **foot**. Elsewhere it sits inside the running head — left on
   verso, right on recto. On two-column back matter the head text is often emitted
   before the number (PDF 283, 291, 293, 295); all four are correct on inspection.
6. **Footnotes are numbered per chapter** and reach into the thirties (footnote 32 on
   printed 24). A bare superscript integer near an item is a footnote marker, not an
   item number.

**Content traps**

7. **Displays share the item counter** (§5 above). This is the single most likely
   source of downstream error.
8. **Display census uncertain.** The TOC pass reported 69 numbered displays in
   printed 1–250; a full text-layer sweep of PDF 21–280 finds 67 parenthesized
   `(c.s.n)` tokens, of which one — `(4.7.17)` — is the Epilogue restatement form and
   not a display, leaving 66 genuine tags (65 within printed 1–250). The gap was not
   reconciled; some tags may live inside picture environments the text layer reorders.
   **Treat the count as approximate; the scheme is certain.** No page range depends
   on it.
9. **Exercise totals were corrected downward** to 206 (Ch. 1–6). If a chapter agent's
   count disagrees with §5, re-read the page rather than trusting either figure.
10. **Second-edition reordering in Chapters 3 and 4.** The Preface states the sections
    of Chs. 3–4 were reordered and that §4.5 is new, so first-edition section numbers
    for those chapters will not line up. Other 2nd-ed. deltas: Proposition 4.3.7
    (mates), Definition 2.4.13 and Proposition 2.4.14 (discrete fibrations promoted
    from exercises), Theorem 4.2.7, new proofs for Lemmas 3.4.5/3.4.6, restated
    Lemma 3.7.1 / Prop 5.3.3 / Prop 6.5.9, new Examples 6.1.3 and 6.2.15, improved
    Corollary 2.2.8 and Theorem 3.8.1.
11. **Unnumbered structures.** Every chapter opener and many section openers carry a
    flush-right epigraph block separated from its attribution by a horizontal rule
    (Eilenberg–Mac Lane at Ch. 1, Mazur at Ch. 2, Mac Lane at Chs. 3/6, Kan at Ch. 4,
    Wadler/Iry at Ch. 5, Freyd at §1.4). These are not items. Chapters also open with
    several paragraphs of unnumbered roadmap prose before §1 begins — which can
    nonetheless contain a numbered display, e.g. (1.0.1).
12. **Notation.** Yoneda embeddings use the hiragana character (font `dmjhira`,
    renders correctly); identities are `id` not `1`; the action groupoid is `X//G`;
    constant diagrams use an explicit Δ; composition is juxtaposition (`gf`), with
    `g · f` used occasionally. Kind names in small caps may extract with inconsistent
    casing.
13. **Do not cite these folios against the Dover/AMS printing** (§1). This is a
    recompiled author's copy with its own pagination.
14. **Chapter label form.** Chapters are labelled in arabic in the book (`CHAPTER 1`)
    and the Epilogue by letter (`E.1`–`E.5`). The `roman` launch field is emitted as
    `"1"`…`"6"`, `"E"` accordingly. If the pipeline requires true roman numerals, map
    I–VI, but do **not** renumber the Epilogue as VII — every internal cross-reference
    uses `E`.
