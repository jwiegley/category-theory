**Correction to the QA record.** `judgement-calls.md:263-264` and `plan-tail-432-1038.md:257` (J34)
describe the pair as "#422 the general J-indexed statement; #737 the discrete-diagram case". That is
not accurate. **Both core statements are the discrete/family case.** #422 carries the
arbitrary-diagram generalisation only as a *later appended checkbox* (Seven Sketches §6.2.3):

> - [ ] (from Seven Sketches §6.2.3) In a preorder's thin category, the colimit of an arbitrary diagram
>       is the join of its objects — hence pushouts exist wherever binary joins do, and the pushout of
>       any span coincides with the coproduct of its two feet.

So the containment is not "general ⊃ special". It is **identical statement, #422 strictly stronger**:
#422 proves an `iff` ("exactly", "in both directions") where #737 proves a one-way "is", and #422
additionally covers the nullary/binary specialisations, the arbitrary-diagram extension, and
completeness. Nothing in #737's item 2 lies outside #422.

Notably, the extra strength is **exactly what #737 needs**: to instantiate
`left_adjoint_preserves_colimits` at a poset and read the conclusion as join-preservation, one needs
*both* legs — a lub yields a colimiting cocone (to feed the theorem) and a colimiting cocone yields a
lub (to read the output). #737's own one-directional phrasing is arguably under-specified for its own
proof; #422's `iff` form is the right artifact.
