## NUMBERING-CONVENTIONS

- **One shared counter per chapter across all environment kinds.** Verified in chapter 2: Definition 2.1 → Proposition 2.2 → Example 2.3 → Example 2.4 → Example 2.5 in sequence. So "Proposition 2.7" means the 7th numbered environment of chapter 2, not anything section-relative. Consequence for IDs of the form `awodey:<chapter>.<section>:<kind><number>`: the `<number>` is **chapter-scoped**; the `<section>` component must be derived from the item's page position (via this report's section→PDF table), and `<kind>` from the printed label (Definition/Proposition/Lemma/Theorem/Example/Corollary).
- **Named results**: some environments carry parenthetical names, e.g. "Lemma 8.2 (Yoneda)"; prose also names theorems without an environment (e.g. the "Stone Duality Theorem" discussed in §7.3 with Proposition 7.5 as its special case). Record names as aliases, not as ID keys.
- **Displayed equations use a separate (chapter.number) counter** — eq. (1.1) on book p.4 precedes Definition 1.1 on p.5, and eq. (8.1) on p.192 precedes Lemma 8.2. Do not mistake equation numbers for item numbers.
- **Exercises are collected in each chapter's final numbered section** (1.9, 2.9, 3.5, 4.5, 5.7, 6.6, 7.10, 8.9, 9.9, 10.6) and numbered 1, 2, 3, … per chapter, often with lettered parts (a)/(b)/(c). The scheme `awodey:<chapter>:ex<number>` is well-defined; parts can be suffixed `a/b/c` if needed.
- **Unnumbered enumerations exist**: §1.4 "Examples of categories" is a plain numbered list (1., 2., …), not "Example 1.x" environments. Chapter 1 has very few numbered environments; inventory agents should not expect every worked example to carry a stable environment number, especially in chapter 1.
