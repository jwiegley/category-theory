## NUMBERING-CONVENTIONS

- Chapters are Roman numerals I–XII; sections are arabic, numbered per chapter, and titled.
- **Theorems/Propositions/Lemmas/Corollaries restart at 1 within each section** ("Theorem 2" exists in both V.6 and XI.5). Cross-section references use the full form: "Theorem II.7.1", "Proposition 6.2" (same chapter), "§ V.7", "§ 8 below" (same chapter).
- **Many key items are unnumbered** — standalone "**Definition.**" and "**Lemma.**" blocks are common (the adjunction Definition on printed 80, the Yoneda-induced Lemma on printed 91, the comma-projection Lemma on printed 121). Inventory agents cannot rely on numbers alone: for unnumbered items assign a per-section ordinal by order of appearance and record the printed page (e.g. `maclane:IV.1:def1 [p.80]`).
- **Displayed equations** number (1), (2), … per section; referenced as "(1)" locally, "(5.2)" cross-section (section.display), "(IX.5.2)" cross-chapter.
- **Exercises** sit at section ends, numbered 1, 2, 3… per section (seen at end of X.7, printed 250; referenced as "Exercise III.4.4", "Exercise IV.7.4", "Exercise III.5.5"). The index marks them "195 Ex."
- Several chapters end with an unnumbered "**Notes.**" block (IX at printed 232, X at printed 250); XI instead ends with §6 "Perspectives". Notes/Perspectives are historical prose, not inventoriable items.
- The proposed stable ID scheme works: `maclane:<ChapterRoman>.<Section>:<kind><n>` with kinds `thm/prop/lem/cor/def/ex/eq`, plus per-section ordinals for unnumbered defs/lemmas; use `maclane:App:…` for the Appendix (its content is a numbered list of axioms, e.g. "6. Terminal object", "7. Pullbacks", "8. Truth" on printed 289).
